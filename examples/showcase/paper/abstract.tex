\title{No Cash Flows, New Owners:\\ Spot Bitcoin ETFs and the Origins of Co-movement}

\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Did the SEC's January 10, 2024 approval of eleven US spot Bitcoin ETFs move
% src: summary_statistics.json#etf.n_spot_funds_approved
Bitcoin into the conventional risk-asset complex? The approval changed who
could hold Bitcoin and in what wrapper; it changed nothing about the asset,
which has no cash flows and hence no fundamentals that could have grown more
correlated with stocks. We estimate a difference-in-differences on monthly
equity co-movement in a panel of ten cryptocurrencies over 2021--2025 ---
% src: summary_statistics.json#n_units
% src: summary_statistics.json#raw_contrast.SPY.control_group_mean.n_control_coins
Bitcoin against nine that never received a US spot ETF --- with coin and
calendar-month fixed effects and randomization inference over placebo assets
and dates, because there is one treated unit.
% src: estimation_results.json#main.treated_units
Bitcoin's correlation with the S\&P 500 changed by
% src: estimation_results.json#main.effect_in_correlation_units
$-0.018$ against a pre-listing level of
% src: estimation_results.json#main.rho_pre_treatment_mean
$0.382$, and its equity beta by
% src: robustness_results.json#outcome_beta.coefficients.d_etf_listed.estimate
$-0.189$ against a pre-listing
% src: summary_statistics.json#raw_contrast.SPY.by_asset.BTC.mean_beta_pre
$1.23$ --- neither distinguishable from the placebo distribution, and both of
the wrong sign for integration. What did move is relative volatility:
Bitcoin's log volatility ratio to the equity market shifts by
% src: robustness_results.json#outcome_ln_sigma_ratio.coefficients.d_etf_listed.estimate
$-0.113$, roughly 11 percent,
% computed: exp(-0.11281474) - 1 = -0.107, i.e. a fall of about 11 percent
against the controls. With 80\% power the design could only have detected
% src: estimation_results.json#power_analysis.power
correlation increases above
% src: estimation_results.json#power_analysis.mde_correlation_units_ri
$0.148$; it excludes increases above
% src: estimation_results.json#power_analysis.ri_ci95_upper_correlation_units
$0.086$. Spot ETFs changed how volatile Bitcoin is relative to stocks, not how
much it moves with them.
