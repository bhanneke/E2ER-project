\title{No Cash Flows, New Owners:\\ Spot Bitcoin ETFs and the Origins of Co-movement}

\begin{abstract}
\noindent In January 2024 the SEC approved US spot Bitcoin exchange-traded products, widening the set of investors who can conveniently hold an asset with no cash flows. We ask whether this change in the investor base altered Bitcoin's co-movement with US equities. We compare Bitcoin with nine cryptocurrencies that never received such a product, using monthly correlations with the S\&P 500 from 2021 and randomization inference suited to a single treated asset. Bitcoin's equity correlation changed by no more than chance would produce: relative to the controls it moved by under two correlation points, in the opposite direction from the broadened-access prediction.
